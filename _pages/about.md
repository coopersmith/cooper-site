# Cooper Smith

I'm a product design leader in Brooklyn, NY. For more than a decade I've built products and design teams at Foursquare, Twitter, Lyft, Sorare and [Asana](https://asana.com/features).

## Asana

Designing Pages, Meetings and mobile for Agentic Work Management  
_2024 - Present · Director of Product Design_

I lead design across Mobile, Pages and Meetings, the newest parts of Asana's Agentic Work Management. Before that I led design for Reporting, Resource Management, Goals and Portfolios.

## Sorare

Designing fantasy sports games built on digital collectibles  
_2022 - 2023 · Design Leadership_

Built a product design team from the ground up in Paris and New York, and served as design lead for Sorare NBA.

## Lyft

Designing the core rider experience  
_2018 - 2022 · Product Design, Design Management_

Joined as a designer, then managed a small rider team, and by the time I left managed design for the full core rider experience, along with the mapping team.

## Twitter

Designing for information quality  
_2015 - 2018 · Product Design_

Worked to [combat misinformation](https://www.nytimes.com) on Twitter following the 2016 election.

## Foursquare

Designing check-ins, tips and messaging  
_2012 - 2015 · Product Design_

Where I cut my teeth in product design.

## Before that

- Visualized fitness tracking data, culminating with designing a full-page spread for [WIRED UK](https://cargocollective.com) [2011]
- Completed the 18th grade at the School of Visual Arts, receiving my master's in Interaction Design [2010-2012]
- Grown in Colorado [1985-2010]
