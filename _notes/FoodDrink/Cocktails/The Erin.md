First discovered at [[Long Island Bar]] with [[Adrian Philips]] on [[2025-07-23]]. First made at home [[2025-07-26]].

## Ingredients

- 2 oz [[Rye whiskey]] (e.g., Rittenhouse, Ragtime Rye)
- 1/2 oz [[China China]] (Bigallet China-China Amer; can use Amer Picon)
- 1/2 oz [[Suze]] (bittersweet gentian apéritif)
- 1/2 oz [[Sweet Vermouth]] (traditionally equal parts Cinzano Rosso & Carpano Antica)
- 5 dashes [[allspice dram]]

## Directions

1. Stir all ingredients with ice ~10 seconds.
2. Strain into a rocks glass over a large ice cube.
3. Garnish: express both lemon and orange peels over the drink.

## Notes

- I didn't have China China so I substituted CioCiaro amaro.
- I didn't have allspice dram so I did 2 dashes Angostura, 2 dashes Pequods, 2 dashes cardamom bitters.
