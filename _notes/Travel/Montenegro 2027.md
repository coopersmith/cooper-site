In the summer of 2027, nine of us are renting a house on the Bay of Kotor for about ten days. Three generations, including a toddler and two kids in school. None of us has been to Montenegro before.

If you know the place, I'd like to hear from you.

What I'm after:

- Where to eat
- Boats, and who to call about them
- Days out that work for a three-year-old and a seventy-something at once
- Whether ten days is too long, and where we should go if it is
- What people do with the middle of the day in July heat
- Anything that isn't in the guides
