### 🧌 Ogres Have Layers
### 🥂 **Base Style**

**Martini / Gibson hybrid**

- Two gins: _Nikka Coffey Gin_ (Japanese, clean and citrusy) and _Isolation Proof Ramp Gin_ (botanical, garlicky/onion-like notes from ramps).
    
- _La Quintinye Vermouth_ (aromatic French vermouth, slightly sweet and herbal).  
    → This combination aligns with the **spirit-forward Martini family** — elegant, boozy, stirred, and served up.
    

---

### 🍸 **Flavor Layering / Twist**

The add-ins — _caramelized onion, mastiha, carrot, butter, olive_ — turn it into a **culinary-style savory Martini**:

- **Caramelized onion + butter** → umami richness, echoing browned flavors.
    
- **Mastiha** → resinous Greek spirit (like pine and herbs), gives a mystical forest note.
    
- **Carrot** → subtle vegetal sweetness, adding “layered” flavor.
    
- **Olive** → classic Martini garnish element, grounding it in the familiar.
    

---

### 🧅 **In summary**

> “Ogres Have Layers” = **a layered, savory Martini** with culinary depth — think of it as a _buttered Gibson_ that explores vegetal and umami flavors, nodding to the “layers” of an ogre (and of flavor).



### 🍕 **Cold Pizza**

**Don Fulano Blanco Tequila**  
**Parmigiano Reggiano, burnt toast, tomato, basil, honey, egg white**

---

### 🥂 **Base Style**

**Tequila Sour / Bloody Maria Hybrid**  
Spirit-forward and savory, balanced by acid and foam — built like a sour (tequila, honey, egg white), but riffing on the _Bloody Mary_ family through its tomato and basil notes.

---

### 🍸 **Flavor Layering / Twist**

- **Tequila (Don Fulano Blanco)** → crisp, vegetal agave base.
    
- **Parmigiano Reggiano + burnt toast** → smoky umami, evokes crust and cheese.
    
- **Tomato + basil** → bright Italian flavor pairing — the “pizza” core.
    
- **Honey + egg white** → smooth, textural balance.  
    → Likely shaken and strained, resulting in a silky, foamy sour that _smells like a wood-fired margherita_.
    

---

### 🔥 **In summary**

> “Cold Pizza” = **a savory tequila sour with Italian pantry notes**, capturing the nostalgia of leftover pizza — smoky, herbal, and slightly sweet.

---

### 🍛 **Thai Curry**

**Sonbi Gin**  
**Ilegal Joven Mezcal**  
**green curry, lime**

---

### 🥂 **Base Style**

**Smoky Gimlet / Southside hybrid**  
Built on the gin-mezcal duo (herbal meets smoke), balanced with citrus, and spiced like a Thai kitchen.

---

### 🍸 **Flavor Layering / Twist**

- **Sonbi Gin** → bright, floral, clean backbone.
    
- **Ilegal Joven Mezcal** → smoke and depth.
    
- **Green curry** → creamy coconut, chili, lemongrass, and kaffir lime — exotic complexity.
    
- **Lime** → acidity to balance fat and spice.  
    → Likely clarified or fat-washed to keep texture clean while retaining aroma.
    

---

### 🧡 **In summary**

> “Thai Curry” = **a tropical, smoky, and aromatic gimlet** — like sipping a cocktail version of green curry with citrus and mezcal smoke.

### 🥗 **Waldorf Salad**

**Dewar’s 12yr Whisky**  
**Laphroaig 10yr Whisky**  
**celery, kale, apple, soda, walnut bitters**

---

### 🥂 **Base Style**

**Highball / Whisky Collins riff**  
A light, effervescent whisky cocktail playing on the structure of a highball but flavored like the classic _Waldorf Salad_.

---

### 🍸 **Flavor Layering / Twist**

- **Dewar’s + Laphroaig** → balanced smoke and malt — sweet/salty contrast.
    
- **Celery + kale** → green bitterness and vegetal edge.
    
- **Apple + soda** → crisp and refreshing, sparkling lift.
    
- **Walnut bitters** → earthy, nutty depth.  
    → Served tall and fizzy — light but savory, vegetal, and smoky.
    

---

### 🍏 **In summary**

> “Waldorf Salad” = **a Scotch highball inspired by the iconic salad**, refreshing and smoky with herbal, apple, and nutty notes.

**Teeling Irish Whiskey**  
**coffee butter, corn, walnut, wild mushroom, microwaved coppa**

---

### 🥂 **Base Style**

**Irish Coffee / Fat-washed Old Fashioned hybrid**  
Rich, spirit-forward, with savory undertones — a Southern breakfast plate reimagined as a whiskey cocktail.

---

### 🍸 **Flavor Layering / Twist**

- **Teeling Irish Whiskey** → bright grain sweetness.
    
- **Coffee butter** → fat-washed richness, evokes diner coffee and gravy.
    
- **Corn + walnut** → sweet-nutty Southern base.
    
- **Wild mushroom + coppa** → savory umami, adding “gravy” depth.  
    → Possibly stirred and served over a large cube — rich, earthy, and comforting.
    

---

### ☕ **In summary**

> “Red Eye Gravy” = **a Southern-style Irish Coffee–Old Fashioned hybrid**, buttery, smoky, and umami — the flavor of a breakfast plate in cocktail form.
